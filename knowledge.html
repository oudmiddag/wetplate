Formulas

Salted collodion (general purpose)

A balanced starting recipe for portraits and general work; age 24h before use.

  • Collodion (USP, 4%)100 ml
  • Cadmium bromide0.8 g
  • Ammonium iodide0.6 g
  • Potassium iodide0.4 g
Source: Book Quinn Jacobson — Chemical Pictures ↗ · last reviewed by M. Sato, Apr 2026

Standard iron developer

Ferrous sulfate developer for tintypes; adjust acetic acid for temperature.

  • Ferrous sulfate15 g
  • Acetic acid (glacial)12 ml
  • Alcohol8 ml
  • Distilled waterto 250 ml
Source: Book The Silver Sunbeam (Towler, 1864), adapted ↗ · last reviewed by L. Meyer, Mar 2026

Glossary

📖

Ambrotype

A collodion positive on glass, backed with black to read as a positive image.

Source:ReferenceAmbrotype — overview & citations ↗
📖

Tintype (ferrotype)

A collodion positive made on a blackened metal plate, robust and inexpensive.

Source:ReferenceTintype (ferrotype) — overview & citations ↗
📖

Silver bath

The silver nitrate solution that sensitises the coated plate; needs regular testing and sunning.

Source:VideoBorut Peterlin — silver bath & chemistry ↗
📖

Collodion

A syrupy solution of nitrocellulose in ether and alcohol. Salted with iodides and bromides, it forms the light-sensitive film carrier that is poured onto the plate before sensitising.

Source:ReferenceCollodion process — overview & citations ↗

Troubleshooting

"Oysters" (crescent-shaped clearings)

Usually contamination or a dirty plate. Re-clean the plate and check the silver bath for organic build-up; filter and sun if needed.

Source:ForumPhotrio — Wet Plate Collodion subforum ↗· reviewed by P. Singh

Comet trails

Particles dragged during development. Filter your developer and pour in one smooth motion; check for dust on the plate edge.

Source:BlogMarkus Hofstätter — studio notes ↗

Fogging / chemical veil

A grey veil over the image, often from an old or contaminated silver bath, over-iodised collodion, or too much free silver. Test the bath's specific gravity and acidity, sun it to drop out organics, then filter before re-use.

Source:ForumPhotrio — silver bath troubleshooting ↗
