About the hub

One open home for the collodion craft

Wet plate knowledge is scattered across old forums, social posts and out-of-print books. Wetplate Hub brings it together — free, searchable and run by the community.

Our mission

To be the global central hub for wet plate collodion photography: a single, welcoming place where anyone — from absolute beginner to seasoned practitioner — can learn the process, see great work, ask questions and connect with photographers near them.

We believe the craft thrives when knowledge is shared openly. Everything here is built and maintained by volunteers, with no ads and no paywalls.

How to take part

Create a free profile to share your plates in the gallery, post and answer questions in the forum, list yourself in the directory, and submit tutorials or events. Every contribution helps the next person learn.
