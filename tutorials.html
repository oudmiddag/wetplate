The wet plate collodion process, step by step

The whole sequence happens in roughly fifteen minutes — the plate must be coated, exposed and developed while still wet.

Flow the collodion

Pour salted collodion evenly across a clean glass or metal plate and drain the excess.

Sensitise in silver nitrate

Immerse the coated plate in the silver bath for 3–5 minutes to make it light-sensitive.

Load & expose

Transfer the wet plate to the holder in the dark and make the exposure in-camera.

Develop

Pour developer over the plate, watch the image rise, then rinse to stop development.

Fix, wash & varnish

Clear in fixer, wash thoroughly, dry and seal with warm sandarac varnish.
